1. How We Process Information

To provide photography, image enhancement, text recognition, and read-aloud features, the App processes the following information on your device:

Information or Data Purpose of Processing How It Is Processed and Stored
Live camera images and captured images To provide a live preview, image enhancement, photography, and OCR text recognition. Processed locally on your device and primarily stored temporarily in memory. The App does not automatically save captured images to your photo library or upload them to a server.
Images you select through the system photo picker To provide image enhancement and OCR text recognition. The App accesses only the images you explicitly select and does not request permission to read your entire photo library. Images are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to a server.
Text produced by OCR To display recognition results, adjust font size, copy text, and read text aloud. Stored only in memory during the current App session. The App does not create a recognition-history database or upload the text to our servers.
App preferences To remember the interface font size, reading font size, and whether you have completed the first-use guide. Stored in the App’s private local storage until you clear the App’s data or uninstall it.
Local runtime logs To diagnose OCR, camera, or image-processing failures. The current implementation may record processing duration, the number of recognized regions, and error information in the device’s system logs. It does not intentionally log complete images or the full recognized text, and the App does not upload these logs.

2. Device Permissions

Camera permission (CAMERA): Used to display a live camera image, take photos, enhance images, and recognize text. The App accesses the camera only after you use a relevant feature and grant permission. You can revoke permission at any time under “Settings—Apps—VisionAid—Permissions” on your device. After permission is revoked, camera-related features will no longer work, but features that do not rely on the camera, such as importing images through the system photo picker, will remain available.

The current release configuration does not enable internet permission. If a future version enables network features or changes how information is processed, we will update this Policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration before the feature is released, and we will obtain your separate consent when required by law or platform policy.

3. Local Processing, Collection, and Sharing

The App uses locally integrated EyeAlgo image-enhancement and OCR components to process images and text. Based on the code and permission configuration of the current version, these components run on your device. The App does not use them to transmit your images or recognition results to the EyeAlgo provider or any other server.

We do not operate a backend service that receives data from the App. We do not collect or share your personal information, photos, recognized text, device identifiers, precise location, contacts, financial information, or App usage records. The App does not contain advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs.

4. System Features and External Services

When you actively use the following features, data may be further processed by the Android system or other apps selected on your device:

  • Photo picker: Browsing images and selecting which images to authorize are handled by the Android system or the media provider configured by the system. The App can read only the images you explicitly select and authorize.
  • System clipboard: When you tap “Copy,” the recognized text is written to the system clipboard. Clipboard contents are managed by the Android system and may be read by other apps that you have permitted to access the clipboard. You can remove the text by copying other content or using a clearing feature provided by the system.
  • Text-to-speech (TTS): When you use “Read Aloud” or enable “Auto Read Aloud,” the recognized text is sent to the TTS engine currently selected on your device to generate speech. The TTS engine may be provided by the device manufacturer or a third party. Whether it connects to the internet, how it stores data, and how it protects data depend on that engine’s settings and privacy policy. You can select or change the TTS engine in system settings, or choose not to use the read-aloud feature.
  • Android backup and device migration: If you enable system backup or device migration on your device, the Android system may back up or migrate the App’s local preferences and private files according to your system settings. This process is controlled by your operating system or backup service provider, and we cannot access the backup copies it stores.
  • Google Play: When you download or update the App through Google Play, Google may process information related to downloads, devices, security, and diagnostics in accordance with its own privacy policy. This information is not collected directly by the App.

5. Information Retention and Deletion

  • Live camera images, captured or imported images, enhanced results, and OCR text are primarily processed temporarily in memory. They are generally released when the content is replaced, the relevant screen is closed, or the App process ends.
  • App preferences, such as font sizes and first-use guide status, remain stored locally on your device until you clear the App’s data or uninstall it.
  • To provide offline OCR, the App installs model files included in the installation package into the App’s private directory. These models do not contain your personal information and are deleted when you clear the App’s data or uninstall it.
  • Data written to the system clipboard or sent to a TTS engine is managed by the applicable system feature or service. You should manage and delete that data through system settings or the methods provided by the relevant service.

The App does not offer account registration and does not create user profiles on our servers. Therefore, there is no account or server-side user data for which you need to request deletion from us. To delete data stored by the App on your device, you can select “Clear storage/Clear data” on the Android App info screen or uninstall the App. Copies stored in a system backup should be managed through your system account or backup-service settings.

6. Information Security

We take measures appropriate to the App’s current data-processing practices to protect information. These measures include storing local preferences in the Android app sandbox, requesting camera permission only when needed, using the system photo picker to limit image access to the minimum necessary scope, and disabling internet permission in the current version. Please note that no device or software environment can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should also protect access to your device and carefully choose your TTS engine and the apps that can access the clipboard.

7. Protection of Minors

The App is an assistive reading tool intended for general users and is not a service directed specifically at children. Minors should use the App under the guidance of a parent or other guardian. Because the App does not collect user data on our servers, we do not create profiles based on users’ ages. If we determine that future features involve minors’ personal information, we will provide appropriate notice, obtain consent, and implement protective measures as required by law.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You may choose not to grant camera permission or may revoke it, choose not to select any images from your photo library, choose not to use the copy or read-aloud features, and clear the App’s data or uninstall the App at any time. Because the current version does not collect user data on a server, we generally have no server records that can be used to identify you or respond to a request for a copy of your data.

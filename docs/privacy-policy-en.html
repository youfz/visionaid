VisionAid Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026
Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the VisionAid Android application (package name: com.you.visionaid, the “App”). The developer and operator of the App are both lanhunff (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Policy explains how the App accesses, processes, stores, shares, and protects information related to you.

Summary: The current version requires no registration or sign-in, contains no advertising, behavior-analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not request internet permission. Camera images, images you select, image enhancement, and OCR are processed locally on your device. The App does not upload this content to our servers or EyeAlgo servers. When you actively use system text-to-speech, the clipboard, the photo picker, or system backup, the relevant data may be processed by Android, your device manufacturer, or a third-party service you select, as described below.

1. Information We Access and Process

InformationPurposeProcessing and Retention
Live camera images and captured images To provide live preview, image enhancement, image capture, and OCR text recognition. Processed only on your device and held primarily temporarily in memory. The App does not automatically save images to your photo library or upload them to a server.
Images selected through the system photo picker and their orientation metadata To display images correctly and provide image enhancement and OCR. The App can access only images you explicitly select and authorize, not your entire photo library. Content is processed temporarily on your device and is not uploaded to a server.
Text produced by OCR To display and format results, adjust text size, copy text, and read text aloud. Held in memory in the current App process. The current version does not create a recognition-history database or upload text to our servers.
Local App settings To remember App and reading text sizes, completion of first-use onboarding, and whether camera permission was previously requested. Stored in the App's private SharedPreferences, generally until you clear the App's data or uninstall it. System backup may retain a separate copy.
Local device runtime logs To diagnose OCR, camera, and image-processing problems. Android system logs may contain processing duration, recognized-region counts, and error information. The App does not intentionally log complete images or full recognized text and does not upload these logs.

2. Device Permissions

Camera permission (CAMERA): Used to display and enhance a live camera feed, capture images, and recognize text in them. The App can access the camera only after you grant permission. You may revoke it at any time in Android's App permission settings. Camera features will then stop working, while features that do not depend on the camera, such as importing an image through the system photo picker, will remain available.

The current version does not request photo-library, microphone, location, contacts, phone, advertising-identifier, or internet permission. The system photo picker gives the App read access only to images you select.

3. Local Processing, Data Collection, and Sharing

The App integrates EyeAlgo image-enhancement and OCR components. Based on the current implementation and permission configuration, these components run on your device. We do not operate a backend that receives App data, and the App does not use these components to send images or recognition results to the EyeAlgo provider or any other server.

The current version does not transmit personal information, images, recognized text, device identifiers, location, contacts, financial information, or App activity from the App to us or third-party servers. Accordingly, under Google Play's Data safety definition of “collection,” the App does not collect or share user data. We do not sell user data.

4. System Features and External Services

The following processing occurs only when you enable the relevant system feature or take the relevant action:

  • Photo picker: Browsing and authorization are handled by Android or the media provider configured on your device. The App receives access only to images you select.
  • System clipboard: When you tap “Copy,” recognized text is placed on the system clipboard. Android manages the clipboard, and other apps with applicable access may read it. You can replace it by copying other content or clear it using a system feature.
  • Text-to-speech (TTS): When you use read-aloud or automatic reading, recognized text is provided to the TTS engine currently selected on your device to generate speech. That engine may be supplied by your device manufacturer or a third party and may process data online under its own settings and privacy policy. You can select or replace the TTS engine in system settings or choose not to use read-aloud features.
  • Android backup and device migration: Because the App permits system backup, Android or a backup service may back up or migrate local preferences and private App files when you enable backup or migration. The relevant provider controls this process; we cannot access copies it stores.
  • Google Play: When you download or update the App through Google Play, Google may process download, device, security, and diagnostic information under its own privacy policy. The App does not directly collect that information.

5. Retention and Deletion

  • Live camera images, captured or imported images, enhanced results, and OCR text are processed primarily temporarily in memory and are generally released when content is replaced, the relevant screen closes, or the App process ends.
  • App settings remain locally on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.
  • Models required for offline OCR are installed from the App package into the App's private directory. They do not contain your personal information and are deleted when you clear App data or uninstall the App.
  • Data in the clipboard, TTS engine, or system backup is managed by the relevant system feature or service. Use the corresponding system or service settings to manage and delete it.

The App does not offer accounts, so there is no account or server-side user profile for us to delete. To delete data stored by the App on your device, choose “Clear storage/Clear data” on the Android App info screen or uninstall the App.

6. Information Security

We use measures appropriate to the App's current processing, including storing local settings in the Android App sandbox, requesting camera permission when needed, limiting image access through the system photo picker, and not enabling internet permission in the current version. No device or software environment can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect access to your device and carefully choose your TTS engine and apps that can access the clipboard.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience assistive reading tool and is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through an App server because the current version does not transmit user data to our servers. Minors should use the App with the guidance of a parent or guardian. If the App later targets children or changes its data practices, we will comply with applicable children's privacy laws and Google Play Families policies and update this Policy and related disclosures before release.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You may decline or revoke camera permission, choose not to select photo-library images, choose not to use copy or read-aloud features, and clear App data or uninstall the App at any time. Where applicable law gives you rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, withdrawal of consent, or complaint, you may contact us at the address below. Because the current version does not collect user data on our servers, we generally have no server record with which to identify you or provide a copy of your data.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when features, technology, laws, or platform rules change. For material changes, we will provide notice through this Policy's publication page or an appropriate in-App method and update the date above. If a future version enables networking, advertising, analytics, accounts, or otherwise changes data handling, we will update this Policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration before release and obtain separate consent where required by law or platform policy.
